# Producer Upload Agreement (v1)

**Effective version:** `v1`

This agreement applies when you upload a resource to Kanjava Music. By accepting at upload time, you agree to the following.

## Terminology

Listings are **royalty-free**, not “copyright-free.” You retain copyright. Buyers receive a usage license with **no ongoing royalty obligation**.

## 1. Rights warranty

You confirm that you created the work yourself or hold full rights to license it. You will not upload uncleared samples, stolen stems, or third-party vocal chops without clearance.

## 2. Grant of license to the platform

You grant Kanjava Music the right to host the resource, stream previews, and sublicense it to buyers under the license tier you select (`royalty_free_standard`, `royalty_free_exclusive`, `cc0`, or `cc_by`).

## 3. Indemnification

You agree to indemnify the platform if your upload infringes someone else’s copyright.

## 4. Irrevocability for sold licenses

Once a buyer downloads under a royalty-free standard or exclusive license, you cannot retroactively pull that license. The platform stores a `license_snapshot_json` at download time to freeze the terms the buyer received.

## 5. Takedown right

The platform may remove a resource and revoke **future** sales if a valid infringement claim is received. Past-sold licenses remain in effect for those buyers.

## 6. Agreement versioning

Your acceptance is recorded with this agreement version (`v1`) and a timestamp. Terms may evolve; new uploads require acceptance of the then-current version.
